# GigShield: Standard Exclusions

| Exclusion Name | Plain-English Explanation | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| War, civil war, and armed conflict | Damages or disruptions resulting from wars or armed conflicts are not covered | Hard |
| Pandemics, epidemics, and government-declared health emergencies | Widespread health crises and resulting lockdowns fall outside standard triggers | Hard |
| Terrorism and acts of political violence | Disruptions caused by acts of terrorism or large-scale political violence | Hard |
| Pre-existing medical conditions affecting ability to work | Inability to work due to previously known personal health issues is not covered | Hard |
| Self-inflicted disruptions | If an individual intentionally chooses not to work despite having the capability | Soft |
| Mechanical breakdown of the worker's vehicle | Everyday vehicle maintenance issues and breakdowns are the worker's responsibility | Hard |
| Acts of God beyond defined triggers | Natural disasters not specifically covered by the policy triggers (e.g. earthquake, lightning) | Hard |
| Fraud or misrepresentation during onboarding | Providing false information or faking documents during registration voids the policy | Hard |
| Claims filed outside the 2-hour post-event window | Delays in claiming exceeding the 2-hour reporting limit following a triggering event | Soft |
